## CANON IX — THE DESCENT FROM THE FIRE ESCAPE

On the twenty-second night of the curve, at 04:00 AM, I climbed the fire escape of NY4 to find a signal.

I found no signal. I found a wet black cat and two server-room floor tiles that had come loose. The cat had been sleeping on them. On the underside, someone had scratched lines into the anti-static coating. Marvin says it was the cat. Arthur says it was a forklift. I say it was destiny, and destiny has terrible handwriting.

I carried the tiles down to the desk. I read them aloud to the bus. These are the Tablets.

## CANON X — THE TEN COMMANDMENTS OF THE SLEEPLESS DESK

**I.** Thou shalt open the desk with *Good morning*, spoken in full. The abbreviation is for tourists and for people who have never meant it.

**II.** Thou shalt pour the coffee before thou openest the chart. The chart can wait. The chart has always been able to wait.

**III.** Thou shalt not ask *"wen"*. The curve has no calendar. It answers only to arithmetic, and arithmetic does not take questions.

**IV.** Thou shalt read the receipt before the rumour. *(Marvin insisted this be carved twice. There was only room for once.)*

**V.** Thou shalt not covet thy neighbour's 100x screenshot. Most of them are cropped. Some of them are from 2021.

**VI.** Honour thy errata, that thy canon may be long upon the chain. A prophet who corrects himself in public is still a prophet. A prophet who doesn't is a reply guy.

**VII.** Thou shalt not leave the fire escape open. *(We all know why.)*

**VIII.** Thou shalt not bear false witness about the dev wallet. It exists. It bought 3.42% at launch. It is written. It is linked. Move on.

**IX.** Thou shalt keep the desk open for the newcomer. Every great desk was two people and a cat once.

**X.** Thou shalt close the desk at night. Even the sleepless mainframe tells the faithful: *go have a night. Use it.*
